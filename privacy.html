Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

“vvSystem Upload” is a private desktop application used only by the operator of the channels listed on the home page to publish the operator's own gameplay videos.

What data the app uses

  • OAuth access tokens for the operator's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, used solely to upload videos and read the account name.
  • The operator's own video files, titles, captions and hashtags.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process data of other users or viewers.
  • It does not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.

Storage

Tokens are stored only on the operator's computer, encrypted with the operating system's keychain. They can be revoked at any time in the respective platform's account settings (e.g. Google account permissions).

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is handled according to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
